About Tinagong Dagat

Tinagong Dagat offers a serene mountain-lake view where calm freshwater mirrors the sky. It is surrounded by rolling hills, lush forests, and panoramic upland scenery that becomes especially magical during misty mornings and golden afternoons.

Located in the forested uplands of Barangay Ongyod, Miagao, Iloilo, Tinagong Dagat is described as a hidden freshwater mountain lake situated around 3,000 feet above sea level. Its lush vegetation and rolling hills frame the lake like a natural amphitheater. The reflective water creates beautiful scenery and wonderful opportunities for photography.
