About Us

Welcome to the official digital gallery of Tinagong Dagat, a hidden gem nestled in the highlands of Barangay Ongyod, Miagao, Iloilo. Our mission is to showcase the breathtaking natural beauty, mystical charm, and cultural significance of this enchanted freshwater lake to local and international travelers alike.

Through photography and community stories, we hope to promote sustainable tourism and inspire deeper appreciation for the rich mountain ecosystems of Miagao. Whether you are an avid trekker mapping out your next adventure or a nature enthusiast exploring from afar, we invite you to discover the magic of our hidden sea.
