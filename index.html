Discover Tinagong Dagat

Tinagong Dagat in Miagao, Iloilo, is a beautiful hidden paradise surrounded by stunning rock formations, clear blue waters, and breathtaking coastal scenery, offering visitors a peaceful and refreshing escape from the busy city where they can enjoy the beauty of nature, explore the unique landscape, appreciate the relaxing sound of the waves, and create unforgettable memories while experiencing one of the hidden natural treasures of Iloilo.
